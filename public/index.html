Keep the free models free

Lattice is free, and it's staying that way. Quark is a small model trained from scratch, and Spark is a fine-tune built on Qwen 2.5 - both released fully open, no subscriptions, no paywalled features. The GPUs that run them aren't free though. If you get something useful out of Lattice, a few dollars on Ko-fi genuinely helps keep the free models free - and helps train whatever comes next. No pressure though - there's no need to donate. Free means free either way.
